Contraction Timer

How to use this app & the 5-1-1 rule

Timing a contraction

Tap Start Contraction the moment a contraction begins, and tap it again (now labeled Stop Contraction) the moment it ends. That's it — the app records the start time, duration, and the interval since your previous contraction automatically.

Reviewing & fixing entries

Use the 1h / 3h / 6h tabs to change how much history is shown. Tap any row in the table to open it, where you can correct the start/end time, set an intensity (Mild / Moderate / Strong / Severe), or delete it.

Labor Notes

Use the free-text box below the history table to log anything that isn't a contraction — when you were admitted, medication given, checks done, and so on. The Insert time button stamps the current time for you.

The 5-1-1 rule

A common rule of thumb for when to head to the hospital or call your provider is the 5-1-1 rule: contractions coming about 5 minutes apart, each lasting about 1 minute, for at least 1 hour.

This is general guidance only, not medical advice. Your provider may give you different numbers to watch for — always follow their instructions, and contact them (or go straight to care) sooner if anything feels wrong, regardless of what this app shows.
